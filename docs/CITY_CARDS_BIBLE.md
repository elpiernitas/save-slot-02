# SAVE SLOT 02 — CITY CARDS Bible

Status: visual/content source of truth for GAME-05+.

## What CITY CARDS are

Small original collectible records of things the game considers noteworthy.

The joke is that the system treats ordinary places/objects/events with the
importance of collectible RPG loot.

They are not:
- combat cards;
- trading cards;
- relationship memories;
- social posts;
- photos pasted into frames;
- a completion grind.

## Tone

A CITY CARD should make Luis think one of:
- "vale, esto está guapo";
- "por qué coño esto tiene carta";
- "ah, claro";
- "qué detalle más tonto."

Not:
- "esto es un test sobre nosotros";
- "esto es Pokémon con nombres cambiados";
- "tengo que conseguir 200."

## Taxonomy

### PLACE
A location or micro-location.

Examples:
- LA MURALLA
- EMPTY TABLE
- CHOLO AT SUNSET

### OBJECT
An ordinary thing elevated by the system.

Examples:
- BOLLARD Lv. ???
- MYSTERY RECEIPT
- SIDRA CRATE

### NPC
A person/animal/ambient character.

Examples:
- SEAGULL WITH INTENT
- RANDY (later)
- WAITRESS (only if useful; avoid carding every NPC)

### EVENT
A moment/state rather than a place.

Examples:
- AFTERNOON IN CIMAVILLA
- LAST SCREENING
- SIDE QUEST UPDATED

### SYSTEM
Meta/game-state cards.

Use rarely.

Examples:
- corrupted save fragment;
- PLAYER 2 related secret card later.

## Numbering

Use a three-digit catalog number for normal cards:
- 001
- 002
- 003

Secret/system cards may use:
- ???
- 0XX
only when the mystery is intentional.

Number is flavour, not completion pressure.

## Rarity meaning

### COMMON
Funny/ordinary discoveries.

### UNCOMMON
Recognisable places or slightly more meaningful discoveries.

### RARE
Unexpected NPCs/events or more elaborate finds.

### HOLO
A visually special moment card; use sparingly.

### SECRET
Hidden narrative/system cards.

Rarity never affects gameplay strength.

## Card face

Recommended information:
- number;
- name;
- kind;
- small original pixel illustration;
- rarity treatment;
- one-line flavour.

Avoid too much text on face.

## Detail view

May show:
- larger art;
- description;
- obtained timestamp;
- discovery location;
- class-specific alternate flavour if already defined.

Do not expose raw save flags.

## Visual system

Base frame:
- navy;
- cream;
- thin warm accent.

Kinds may use subtle accent differences:
- PLACE: coastal green;
- OBJECT: stone/grey;
- NPC: coral;
- EVENT: warm gold;
- SYSTEM: pale cyan/white glitch accent.

Rarity is a secondary treatment, not a rainbow explosion.

HOLO:
- small animated diagonal sheen or palette shimmer;
- reduced motion disables animation.

SECRET:
- minimal system/glitch language;
- no horror aesthetic unless narrative specifically needs it.

## Acquisition cadence

Target for the whole short game:
roughly one meaningful card every 4–8 minutes, with some sections giving none.

Never give three cards back-to-back just for interacting with everything.

## Writing rules

Names:
- short;
- memorable;
- often deadpan.

Flavor:
- one line;
- not exposition;
- no fake sentimentality;
- no direct private-chat quotes by default.

Descriptions:
- optional;
- can be 1–3 sentences in detail view;
- may contain a subtle real reference;
- should still make sense to someone who only knows the game world.

## Originality / IP rule

CITY CARDS may evoke the joy of card collecting.

Do not copy:
- Pokémon framing;
- card backs;
- energy/type symbols;
- rarity stars;
- fonts;
- stat blocks;
- attack names;
- HP;
- booster-pack presentation.

This is its own in-world archive system.
