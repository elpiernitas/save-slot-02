# GAME-07 — Release Scope Cut

Status: contingency plan.

GAME-07 itself cannot be removed.

If schedule pressure is severe, use this minimum.

## Keep

- boss scene;
- 3 stabiliser nodes;
- two hazard types;
- three-strike integrity;
- retry;
- assist;
- boss persistence;
- PLAYER 2 reveal;
- Manu recognisable asset;
- 2/2 cooperative gate;
- route to GAME-08.

## Cut

- class-specific mechanical modifiers;
- elaborate background animation;
- more than two hazard families;
- optional boss/card reward;
- extra reveal dialogue;
- complex scripted camera;
- custom boss music;
- nonessential particle effects.

## Minimum timing

Boss:
60–75 s successful run.

Reveal + co-op:
45–70 s.

The whole phase can therefore remain under ~3 minutes on a clean run.

## Quality protection

Do not solve schedule pressure by:
- using a generic Manu sprite;
- making telegraphs unreadable;
- removing assist;
- turning reveal into plain text only.

Reduce quantity/effects, not recognition or usability.
